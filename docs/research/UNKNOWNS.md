# Unknowns

Record unresolved questions explicitly. Link partial or conflicting evidence when it exists.

## Unknown template

```markdown
## UNK-NNN — Open question

- Classification: UNKNOWN
- Source: <SRC-NNN, multiple IDs, or UNKNOWN>
- Repository: <URL or UNKNOWN>
- Commit SHA: <full SHA or UNKNOWN>
- Evidence location: <file/path :: function/symbol/section, or UNKNOWN>
- Kindle model: <model or UNKNOWN>
- Firmware: <version or UNKNOWN>
- Confidence: <high | medium | low confidence that the question is correctly framed>
- Relevance: <why resolving this matters to StayInk>
- Current evidence: <known limits, conflicts, or NONE>
- Resolution criteria: <evidence or observation that would answer the question>
```

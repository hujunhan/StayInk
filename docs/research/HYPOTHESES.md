# Hypotheses

Record inferences as testable hypotheses, not facts. Prefer tests that are read-only or reversible.

## Hypothesis template

```markdown
## HYP-NNN — Testable statement

- Classification: INFERENCE
- Source: <SRC-NNN, multiple IDs, or UNKNOWN>
- Repository: <URL or UNKNOWN>
- Commit SHA: <full SHA or UNKNOWN>
- Evidence location: <file/path :: function/symbol/section, or UNKNOWN>
- Kindle model: <model or UNKNOWN>
- Firmware: <version or UNKNOWN>
- Confidence: <high | medium | low>
- Relevance: <why this matters to StayInk>
- Reasoning: <facts and assumptions leading to the hypothesis>
- Prediction: <observable result if correct>
- Test: <smallest safe test and its success criteria>
- Falsified by: <observable contradictory result>
- Status: <untested | supported | falsified>
```

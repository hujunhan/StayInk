# Facts

Only record claims directly supported by evidence. A fact applies only to the listed model, firmware, and source scope.

## Fact template

```markdown
## FACT-NNN — Narrow factual statement

- Classification: FACT
- Source: <SRC-NNN>
- Repository: <URL or UNKNOWN>
- Commit SHA: <full SHA or UNKNOWN>
- Evidence location: <file/path :: function/symbol/section, or UNKNOWN>
- Kindle model: <model or UNKNOWN>
- Firmware: <version or UNKNOWN>
- Confidence: <high | medium | low>
- Relevance: <why this matters to StayInk>
- Evidence: <concise observation or paraphrase>
- Verification: <how the claim was checked>
```
